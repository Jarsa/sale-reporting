This module adds a second printout to quotations and sale orders,
*Quotation / Order (Internal)*, meant to be printed by the internal
team.

It is a minimalist and compact document:

- Internal layout (no company logo, no address blocks, no decorative
  elements) and a paper format with reduced margins.
- Small font size and one row per product line.
- No product images, even when another module (for example
  ``sale_order_report_product_image``) adds them to the customer
  printout.

Columns: line number, internal reference, short description (product
name without the internal reference), quantity, unit of measure, unit
price, discount (only when at least one line has a discount) and
subtotal. Sections and notes are printed as single rows. Untaxed amount,
taxes and total are shown at the end.

The customer printout (*Quotation / Order*) is not modified.

Configuration
=============

The report uses the *Sale Order Compact* paper format (Letter, reduced
margins). To use another paper size go to *Settings > Technical >
Reporting > Paper Format* and edit it, or change it on the report
action.

Usage
=====

1. Go to *Sales > Orders > Quotations* (or *Orders*) and open a record.
2. Click on *Print > Quotation / Order (Internal)*.
